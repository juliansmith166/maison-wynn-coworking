The Fifth Layer · Business Development

We don't just fill your space. We grow your tenants.

Most coworking operators sell desks, then hope tenants stick around. We do something different. We become the business development engine inside your space, helping each tenant define their ideal customer, build a real pipeline, and book revenue. When tenants grow, retention takes care of itself.

The hardest part of running a coworking space isn't filling it. It's keeping the people inside long enough to renew. The tenants who renew aren't the ones with the best Wi-Fi. They're the ones whose business is growing inside your four walls.

That's why we built Business Development as a stand-alone offering. We sit with each member, define their ideal customer profile, build a verified contact list, set up campaigns inside Apollo, and run multi-touch sequences that drive real meetings. Operators get a measurable lift in member outcomes. Tenants get the one thing they can't get anywhere else, revenue.

Run it as a tenant amenity, sell it as a premium add-on, or bundle it into a flagship membership tier. Either way, you become the coworking brand that grows businesses, not just leases.

Operator FAQ

The questions we hear most.

A quick read for the practical questions that come up before, during, and after a pilot. If yours isn't here, the walk-through is the place to ask it.

Can we start with just one pillar, or do we have to take all four? +

Start with one. Most boutique operators begin with Activation Essentials or Tenant Success Concierge as a 30-day pilot, then add pillars as they prove out. Multi-location operators usually start with two pillars across one flagship site before standardizing across the portfolio. We never push the full stack on a first engagement.

We're a national operator. Do you work outside DC? +

Yes. Maison Wynn is headquartered in Washington, DC and works directly with operators across the DMV, but we run flagship engagements nationally. For multi-location rollouts we deploy a dedicated lead per region and standardize the playbook from DC. Travel and on-site cadence are built into the regional tier.

How quickly do operators see a measurable lift? +

Tour-conversion and tenant satisfaction shifts show up inside the first 30 days, on the same pilot. Retention and renewal lift takes a full lease cycle to confirm. We instrument both with a tenant-success scorecard so you can show the numbers to your board, your LP, or your franchise group.

Are you exclusive within a region or building? +

For regional and enterprise tiers, yes. We grant category exclusivity within a defined radius for the duration of the partnership. Boutique pilots are non-exclusive by default, but exclusivity can be added before scaling. We won't run two competing flagships on the same block.

What's the contract length and exit? +

The pilot is a flat 30-day engagement with no auto-renew. Retainers are quarterly with a 60-day exit window. Enterprise rollouts are typically 12 months tied to a specific tenant-outcome scorecard, with a 90-day off-ramp. We earn the next quarter, every quarter.

What if our tenants don't engage with the programming? +

That's the most important question to ask. Our pilot includes a tenant interview pass in week one before any programming drops, so we don't deploy something that doesn't fit the community. If a tenant cohort actively rejects an activation, we replace it the next month, no change order. The point of a pilot is to learn, not to invoice.
